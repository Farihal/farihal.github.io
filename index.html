R3Diver: underwater ROV for rapid search and rescue

2016 · Co-authorIEEE TENCON

A remotely operated vehicle for underwater search and rescue, built for about USD 100 so that rescue teams with small budgets could afford one.

  • PVC frame, four thrusters and 3D-printed propellers.
  • Wired signal control with live video down to 30 m.
  • Automatic human detection in the video feed using HOG and Haar cascade classifiers.

My role: control, thruster design, HMI, vision.

S. A. Fattah, F. Abedin, M. N. Ansary, M. A. Rokib, N. Saha, and C. Shahnaz, “R3Diver: Remote robotic rescue diver for rapid underwater search and rescue operation,” Proc. IEEE TENCON, 2016, pp. 3280–3283. DOI: 10.1109/TENCON.2016.7848658

R3Arm: gesture-controlled robotic arm for remote rescue

2017 · Co-authorIEEE R10-HTC

A low-cost, modular robotic arm for remote rescue work, driven by hand gestures instead of a joystick so an operator can control it naturally from a distance.

  • 3-DOF arm on a mobile base, controlled by finger-mounted accelerometers.
  • Gesture data sent wirelessly over an nRF24L01 link.
  • Live smartphone video lets the operator work without line of sight.

My role: gesture acquisition, firmware, mechanical design.

P. P. Sarker, F. Abedin, and F. N. Shimim, “R3Arm: Gesture controlled robotic arm for remote rescue operation,” Proc. IEEE Region 10 Humanitarian Technology Conf. (R10-HTC), 2017, pp. 428–431. DOI: 10.1109/R10-HTC.2017.8288991

Ride-hailing mobile app for flood rescue operations

2014 · Smarter Planet Challenge · Efficient disaster response

A mobile application that works like a ride-hailing app, except for flood rescue. Stranded people request help from their phones, and the nearest available rescue team is dispatched to them.

  • People caught in a flood send an SOS with their location.
  • The app matches each request with the nearest free rescue team, the way a ride-hailing app matches a rider with a driver.
  • Both sides can follow the rescue as it happens, so teams don't duplicate work and families know help is coming.

Electric motor for rural river boats

2023 – 2024 · M.Sc. thesisFirst author

A 12 kW interior permanent-magnet motor designed as a drop-in replacement for the single-cylinder diesel engines that power boats in rural inland waterways.

>93%efficiency at rated and peak torque (target 92%)
59%less peak cogging torque after applying three-step rotor skew, 1.672 → 0.675 N·m
93.81%energy utilization over the ISO 8178-4 E5 duty cycle
7.85rotor safety factor at 110% of maximum speed
  • Designed in ANSYS Motor-CAD: 48 slots, 8 poles, single layer V-shaped NdFeB magnets and water-jacket cooling, matched to the legacy engines' 2200 rpm operating point.
  • Electromagnetic, mechanical and thermal analysis; peak cogging torque 1.3% of rated torque, torque ripple 7.32%
  • Modeled with 180 mm stator and 114 mm rotor, laminations of M19 steel, an 87 mm stack, a 1 mm air gap, N42UH sintered NdFeB magnets and eight rotor cooling ducts.

F. Abedin and M. Z. R. Khan, “Design and Analysis of a Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motor for Sustainable Rural Water Transportation,” Next Research, 2026, p. 101589. DOI: 10.1016/j.nexres.2026.101589

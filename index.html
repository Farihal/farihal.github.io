01 / Research Focus

Energy Systems &
Intelligent Governance

My research sits at the intersection of electrical machine design, renewable energy systems, and intelligent energy management. My Master's work on permanent magnet synchronous motors for rural water transport — recently published in an Elsevier journal — reflects my commitment to deploying advanced electromechanical systems in resource-constrained contexts. My named contribution to IEEE 2418.5-2025 on blockchain in power systems has deepened my engagement with energy infrastructure governance. At doctoral level, I aim to bridge high-performance electric drive design with AI-assisted optimisation, targeting sustainable energy access in underserved communities.
